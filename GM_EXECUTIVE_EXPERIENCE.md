# GM Executive Experience

The GM role is a distinct executive-governance role. It is not an alias for Admin or Management.

## Navigation

The GM sidebar contains only executive-level destinations:

1. Executive
2. People
3. Capability
4. Governance
5. Quality
6. Operations
7. Administration
8. Reports & Analytics
9. Notifications
10. My Profile
11. QR Verify
12. Sign out

Detailed business pages are opened contextually from these parent workspaces rather than duplicated in the sidebar.

## Executive Command Center

The landing page is designed around exception management:

- Global cross-module search
- Executive KPI row
- Requires GM Attention
- Executive Risk Pulse
- Decision Inbox
- Personal Watchlist
- Direct drill-down into People, Capability, Governance, Quality, Operations and Administration

## GM authority boundary

GM receives organization-wide visibility and Administration manage rights. The baseline does **not** grant implicit `Approve` or `Review` permissions for technical reviews, competency, CRB, QMS or authorization decisions. Those remain with their authorized professional roles.

GM administration covers users, departments, permissions, system settings, audit and recovery governance.

## One source of truth

GM workspaces aggregate authoritative records from existing modules. They do not create duplicate employee, training, competency, authorization, QMS, job, feedback or KPI databases.

The only GM-specific persisted record is `gm_watchlist`, a personal set of references to existing records.

## Page-within-page model

The UX follows:

`Sidebar area → executive workspace → tab/subview → authoritative detailed page → record workspace`

This keeps the sidebar concise while preserving all underlying functionality.
