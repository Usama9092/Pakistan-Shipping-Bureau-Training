# QMS / Technical & Quality Update

## Purpose
Professional QMS governance layer for audits, compliance monitoring, management review and evidence assurance.

## Single-source-of-truth rules
- NCR/CAPA remains the single enterprise corrective-action engine.
- Technical Reviews remain the authoritative technical review records.
- Evidence Review references source records and does not create duplicate file or workflow systems.
- Management Review stores decisions/actions; execution remains in the owning module.

## QMS capabilities
- Quality dashboard
- QMS audit planning and result recording
- Compliance register with review due dates
- Management Review records
- Evidence review and acceptance
- Open NCR/CAPA monitoring
- Audit/governance logging

## Tables
- qms_audits
- qms_compliance_items
- qms_management_reviews
- qms_evidence_reviews

## Permissions
QMS access is limited to Admin, QMR, QMS Auditor, Lead Auditor, Technical Manager and Management in the UI, with underlying permission controls remaining the authoritative enforcement layer.
