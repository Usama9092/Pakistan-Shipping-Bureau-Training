# PERFORMANCE_KPI_UPDATE

## Performance & KPI

KPI is now a derived analytics layer, not a manual metric-entry system. It consumes authoritative records from Training, Competency, Authorization, Technical Reviews, QMS, Job Allocation, Client Feedback and NCR/CAPA.

### Governance snapshot
Privileged users may save an evidence snapshot of the calculated KPI for a period. Snapshots are immutable historical records and do not replace source data.

### Duplicate-prevention rule
No manual entry of surveys, plans, audits, NCR counts, client scores or training compliance is performed on the KPI page. These metrics are derived from existing source modules.
