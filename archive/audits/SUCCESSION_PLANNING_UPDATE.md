# Succession Planning Update

Succession Planning is now a workforce-governance workflow. It identifies critical roles, potential successors, readiness, potential rating, risk and sponsor, while linking to the authoritative Development Plan rather than storing duplicate development actions.

## Key rules
- Employee identity remains authoritative in Users & Employee Profile.
- Development actions remain authoritative in Development Plans.
- Competency and authorization remain authoritative in their own modules.
- Succession stores the relationship to the target role/position and readiness governance.
- Review changes are audited with before/after values and reason.
- No destructive delete workflow is exposed.
