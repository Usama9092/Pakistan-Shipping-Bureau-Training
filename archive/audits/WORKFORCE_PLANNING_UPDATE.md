# Workforce Planning Update

## Purpose
Workforce Planning is now a capacity-and-demand planning module. It does not duplicate employee, training, competency, authorization or succession records.

## Current capacity
Capacity is derived from active users, availability, competency status and approved authorizations.

## Demand forecast
Authorized planning roles can record future demand by period, department and role, with demand basis, priority, required headcount, mitigation plan and notes.

## Gap management
Capacity gaps are calculated from required headcount minus currently available staff. Mitigation should be handled through the existing People, Training & Competency and Authorization workflows.

## Governance
Forecast creation is audited with actor, forecast ID, scope, required headcount, gap and risk. Forecast records are retained for planning history.
