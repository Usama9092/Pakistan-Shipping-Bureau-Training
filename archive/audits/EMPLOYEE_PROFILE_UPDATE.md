# Employee Profile Update

The People & Development area now includes a professional, read-only Employee Profile page.

## Design principles
- One 360-degree view of an employee.
- No duplicate business workflows are created here.
- Training, competency, authorization, performance, documents and history remain owned by their original modules.
- Profile actions navigate to the authoritative module instead of editing duplicate records.
- Credentials and security secrets are never displayed.

## Profile sections
- Overview
- Development
- Training & CPD
- Competency
- Authorization
- Performance & Feedback
- Documents
- History

## Access
- Elevated business roles can search organization-wide profiles.
- Other users are restricted to their own profile.
- User-level history is a filtered view of the immutable Audit Trail.
