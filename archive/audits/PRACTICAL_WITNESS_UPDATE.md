# Practical / Witness Update

The Practical / Witness module is now a controlled evidence workflow.

- Witness and supervised/plan-review evidence remain separate authoritative records.
- Scheduling, assessor identity, scoring, evidence state and verification are tracked.
- Authorized assessors may record evidence; assigned Tutor/Mentor, Trainer, Assigner or designated technical roles may work within their permissions.
- Evidence verification is a separate step and is audited.
- Readiness is calculated from existing Training, Practical/Supervised evidence and Development Plans.
- No competency or authorization record is duplicated here.
- Practical evidence is retained for history and cannot be treated as an authorization approval.
